About this app

Jarvis (the “App”) is a personal integration operated by a single individual (the “Owner”). It is not a public product and is not offered to anyone else.

The App connects an AI assistant that runs on a private server owned and operated by the Owner to the Owner's own Google accounts. None of the App's components are cloud hosted: the assistant, the connector and its credentials all run on that self-hosted server, which keeps them under the Owner's direct control for security and compliance reasons. When the Owner asks the assistant to do something, the App uses Google's APIs to carry it out, for example:

  • summarizing or searching email, and drafting or sending replies the Owner approves;
  • checking availability and creating or updating calendar events;
  • finding, reading, creating and editing files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides.

Access is granted only through Google's standard sign-in and consent screen, and can be revoked at any time (see How to revoke access).

Who can use it: only the Owner. The App does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to the public.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how the App accesses, uses, stores and shares data obtained from Google APIs. The only Google accounts the App is used with are accounts belonging to the Owner.

2. Google data the App accesses

With the Owner's consent on Google's authorization screen, the App may access the following data in the Owner's Google accounts:

Google serviceData accessedWhy
GmailMessages, threads, labels, drafts, attachmentsTo search, summarize, draft, send and organize email at the Owner's request
Google CalendarCalendars and eventsTo read the Owner's schedule and create or update events at the Owner's request
Google DriveFile names, metadata and contentsTo find, read, create, organize and share files at the Owner's request
Google Docs, Sheets, SlidesDocument, spreadsheet and presentation contentsTo read, create and edit documents at the Owner's request
Basic profileEmail address and nameTo identify which of the Owner's accounts is signed in

3. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features described above, at the Owner's direction. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used for any purpose unrelated to the Owner's own requests.

4. Where the data is processed

  • On the Owner's self-hosted server. The App runs entirely on a private server the Owner owns and operates; no part of it is cloud hosted. Data retrieved from Google is processed in memory to complete a request and is not persisted by the App. The only items stored are the OAuth authorization tokens, kept on that server so the App can stay signed in.
  • AI model provider. To answer a request, the relevant portions of retrieved Google data (for example, an email's text) may be sent to the AI language-model provider the Owner has configured for the assistant, solely to generate a response for the Owner. That provider processes the data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google. Actions the Owner requests (such as sending an email or editing a document) are carried out through Google's APIs and stored by Google under Google's terms.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not shared, sold or transferred to anyone except: (a) the AI model provider described above, only as needed to fulfil the Owner's requests; (b) Google, to carry out requested actions; and (c) where required by law.

6. Limited Use disclosure

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google user data obtained by the App is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with the Owner's own access, for security purposes, or as required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

The App does not persist Google user data. Content retrieved from Google is used only to complete the request at hand and is not written to storage. OAuth authorization tokens are stored on the Owner's server until access is revoked or the tokens are deleted.

8. How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by removing “Jarvis”. Revoking access stops the App from reaching that Google account immediately. Stored tokens can also be deleted from the server running the App.

9. Security

Authorization tokens are stored only on the Owner's self-hosted server, protected by operating-system access controls, and access to the App's running service requires authentication. Only the minimum Google permissions needed for the features above are requested.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
